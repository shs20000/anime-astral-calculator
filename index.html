ⓘ How to Use

  1. Enter your current power and select its unit.
  2. Enter the desired power you want to reach.
  3. Enter your Power Per Click (PPC) exactly as shown in-game.
  4. If you have Fast Click, tick it to use 3.66 clicks/sec. Otherwise the calculator uses the base 2.66 clicks/sec.
  5. If you have a qualifying Primordial, tick it to include its 30s ×2 PPC activation every 90s.
  6. Press Calculate to see the time remaining and your progress percentage.
💡 Tip: Your PPC is the power number shown in the bottom-right of Anime Astral.
